Product Label Declaration Rules

Mandatory items that must be printed on pre-packaged commodity labels.

Mandatory Label Items
Required Label Item Description Status
Product Name The common or generic name of the item. Mandatory
Manufacturer Details Full name and complete address of manufacturer, packer, or importer. Mandatory
Net Quantity & Unit Net weight or volume in standard metric units (g, kg, ml, L). Mandatory
Manufacturing & Expiry Dates Month and year of packing and Expiry Date / Best Before declaration. Mandatory
Maximum Retail Price (MRP) MRP in Rupees (₹/Rs.) inclusive of all taxes. Mandatory
Batch / Lot Number Batch, lot, or code number for product tracking. Mandatory
Country of Origin Country where the product was made or imported from. Mandatory
Customer Care Helpline Name, address, phone number, or email for customer complaints. Mandatory
